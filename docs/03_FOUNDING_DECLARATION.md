## Our Resolution

Banjara Language Engine (BLE) is a community-driven digital mission dedicated to preserving, documenting, researching, and developing the Banjara language, literature, culture, history, and traditional knowledge for present and future generations.

This mission is established in the spirit of cooperation, public service, and community participation.

BLE is not merely a software project; it is a long-term language preservation movement.

---

## Founding President

### Late Shri Krishna Nayak Chavhan

Author of:

- Banjara Geethamruth
- Bachkarani

His literary vision and lifelong dedication to the preservation of the Banjara language form the intellectual foundation of this mission.

We respectfully dedicate this project to his memory and his contribution to Banjara language and literature.
